## v0.3.0 - 2026-10-15
### Added
* Redesigned Managed Service, Enterprise and About as plugin block patterns (`forgedseo/page-*`) built from shared section builders in `includes/sections.php`: paper and dark page heroes, pricing card, audience grid, 90-day timeline, ownership split, FAQ accordion, story and principles. An `init` migration (option `forgedseo_core_pages`) points each page body at its pattern.
* Blog index, single post and 404 templates as `forgedseo/template-*` patterns written into the Twenty Twenty-Five database templates: blog cards with pagination and the branded empty state, a post header with featured image, reading column, end CTA and a "Keep reading" row that excludes the current post.
* Published the Privacy Policy and created a Terms of Service page with draft legal copy, and added About, Privacy and Terms links to the footer copyright line.
### Changed
* Home hero, proof, pipeline, why and agents patterns now render through the shared section builders; the Home markup is unchanged.
### Fixed
* Managed Service no longer says there are no fixed prices or sends its CTA to a mailto link; it shows the $1,000/month plan, the 30-day trial and the contact form, matching Home.
